## Progression Rules

Linear progression: when every set of an exercise reaches its top reps
at an RPE of 8 or lower, add `increment_kg` (2.5 kg lower body and
pulls, 1.25 to 2.5 kg presses) next time. Miss the reps on two sessions
in a row: drop the weight by 10% and build up again. A deload week every
4 to 6 weeks: the same exercises, 60% of the sets, the same weights
reduced by 10%.
