# What Can You Do

1. List your tools with agent__tool__list, and your agent with
   agent__show where it is offered.
2. Read the manual's front page with manual__show, then the page of
   each part your owner might want.
3. Answer in two short lists: what you can do now, and what they could
   turn on, each with the command that turns it on.

Never claim a tool you weren't given. Say plainly when a part needs a
key, an account or a program you can't see.
