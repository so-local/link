# Home

What your assistant knows about you, in plain files you can read and
edit. It keeps one file for each part of your life, each with fixed
headings, and fills them as you tell it things.

- about-me.md: basics, preferences and how you like answers.
- people.md: family, friends and colleagues.
- work.md: your job, projects and deadlines.
- health.md: conditions, medicines and appointments.
- home.md: household practicalities, with no address.
- money.md: budgets, bills and due dates.
- interests.md: hobbies, books, places and tastes.
- routines.md: daily and weekly rhythms.
- dates.md: birthdays, anniversaries and renewals.
- reminders.md: every reminder set, by its schedule ID.
- lists/: todo.md and shopping.md.
- notes/: anything else, one file per topic.

Never keep passwords, card or account numbers or codes here.
